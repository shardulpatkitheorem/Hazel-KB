# Open-Question Registry

This is a derived index of unresolved questions shared by Vantage and Theorem.

## Entry format

```markdown
### Q-001 — Short question

- Status: open | blocked | answered
- Owner: Explicit owner or `unknown`
- Raised: YYYY-MM-DD
- Evidence: `iterations/<iteration-id>/meeting-delta.json#<item-id>`
- Blocking: Named decision or change, or `none`
- Answer: Evidence link or `not-yet-answered`
```

Questions remain visible after they are answered and link to the resolving iteration.
